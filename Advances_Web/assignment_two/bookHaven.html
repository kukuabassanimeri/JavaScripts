A Doll's House

Henrik Ibsen

A Doll's House is a three-act play written by Norwegian playwright Henrik Ibsen. It premiered at the Royal Danish Theatre in Copenhagen, Denmark, on 21 December 1879, having been published earlier that month.

Blossom Of Savannah

Henry ole Kulet

Blossoms of the Savannah is a novel by Henry ole Kulet that tells the story of two sisters, Taiyo and Resian, who are on the verge of womanhood and face a conflict between their personal ambitions and the expectation to undergo Female Genital Mutilation (FGM) and early marriage within their Nasila culture.

Campus Diary

JB Omukangala

Campus diary is a romantic novel written by JB Omukangala, It revolves about campus students who do fall in love while on Campus.

Marriage of Dolls

JB Omukangala

Marriage is Dolls is a novel written by JB Omukangala. It address marriage among the populace of Kenya.
